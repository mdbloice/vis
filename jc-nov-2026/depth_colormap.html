Depth painted as colour, and read back as metres

For depth, the model outputs a false-colour image. Each distance corresponds to exactly one colour on a path through the colour cube, so the colour can be converted back into metres. Nearby distances get more of the colour range, because precision matters most up close.

1. From distance to colour

curved distance f(d) = 1 − (1 + d / 10 m)−2   (power transform, λ = −3, c = 10/3)
then f is walked along the cube's edges from black (0 m) to white (∞)

The order of colours along the cube path is an illustrative reconstruction; the paper describes a path along the edges of the RGB cube from black to white.

2. A generated depth image, decoded

Hover over the depth image to read off distances.

Toy scene: road, a person at 5.5 m, a car at 9 m, a pole at 3.5 m, a building at 30 m, sky at infinity. Try adding noise: errors stay small up close and grow with distance, because far distances share a small part of the colour path.
